## 原模块说明

```text
Paired answer-flip audit over an already scored evidence-budget grid.

The unit of resampling is the HotpotQA question.  No model or retriever is
called.  Protocol-invalid local fallbacks are always counted as incorrect in
the primary audit, even when a plaintext recovery happens to contain the gold
answer.
```
